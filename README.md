 LIT Agent

> **Your tiny AI buddy for reading research papers.** 

LIT Agent takes a research paper PDF and turns it into a simple, structured analysis.

Just:

 Upload → Analyze → Understand**

### What it finds

* Summary
* Research problem & objectives
* Methodology
* Datasets & results
* Strengths & limitations
* Research gaps
* Future work
* Conclusion

###  Built with

`Python` · `Streamlit` · `LangChain` · `OpenRouter` · `Pydantic` · `pypdf`

###  How it works

```text
PDF
 ↓
Extract text
 ↓
LLM
 ↓
Structured analysis
 ↓
 Understand the paper
```

This is a little project I'm building while learning more about **LLMs, LangChain and AI agents**.
